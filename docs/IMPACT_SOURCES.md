## Caveats
- **Fallow (nadas):** no 2023–2025 national figure could be checked. The TÜİK data portal and the Ministry table did not load as text. 3.5 M ha is the 2018 figure.
- **Erosion:** 642 Mt is ÇEM's DEMİS model figure for total erosion. The ÇEM PDF itself would not open (SSL certificate error), so it is cited through TEMA. 154 Mt is a different measure: soil carried by rivers.
- **Water:** older DSİ-based figures are 77% (TRT Haber, 2024, with no named source on the page). The 79% figure is the newest official one, from the National Water Plan.
- **Yalova:** the figure is old and its data year is unclear. Use it only with care.
