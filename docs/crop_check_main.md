# MAIN_CROPS parameter check

Checked against `data/crops.js` (MAIN_CROPS, fallow skipped). Done with web search on 2026-11-14.

Caveats:
- The English FAO "Crop Water Information" pages returned HTTP 404 when fetched directly. The FAO figures below come from search-engine extracts of the FAO pages (FR/RU/ZH/AR mirrors of the same text). The URLs listed are the ones the search returned.
- US calendars come from USDA-NASS Agricultural Handbook 628 (Dec 1997), "most active" windows. The text extraction warned that the KS/NE silage and sunflower rows were the least reliable.
- `heatMax` is compared with the month's hottest day, while most thresholds in the literature are for sustained or daily maxima around flowering. A single hot day at the threshold is a warning sign, not proof of yield loss.
